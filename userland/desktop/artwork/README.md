# Keiland artwork

- `zedbsd-boot-splash.png` (1672x941): the boot screen: the glass mark, the word zedBSD, "operating system",
  a dotted spinner, over a bright blurred lake landscape. The picture is the project owner's, provided on
  2026-09-28 for use in the tree ("この画像に限って言えば、ソースツリーに入れてしまってOKです"); its word was
  replaced with the system's name on 2026-10-29: the old word and line were filled from the landscape around
  them, and "zedBSD" (Lato Light) and "operating system" (Lato Regular) were drawn in their place. Lato is
  (c) 2010-2011 Łukasz Dziedzic, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the Debian package fonts-lato 2.015-1);
  the license's terms on the fonts do not reach a picture made with them. It is the design base for the boot
  screen, the wallpaper, the greeter, the lock screen and the file manager's Welcome
  (plan/ws035/kei-identity-design.md). Other reference pictures stay out of the tree.
- The mark (mark.c) takes its motif from the sacred stone rod and stone boat of the Jomon period at the
  Togariishi site, held as the gods of the descendants' prosperity (子孫繁栄).
