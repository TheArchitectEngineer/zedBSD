/*
 * Ask that a numbered IRQ be delivered to the CPUs of a mask.
 *
 * The HAL routes the IRQ to one CPU of the mask that is online (in

 * request and is reported by hal_irq_get_affinity().  A machine that
 * delivers every IRQ to one CPU accepts a mask that includes that CPU

 *

 * controller cannot route the IRQ (a message-signalled IRQ's destination
 * is fixed when it is allocated).  On an error the route and the request
 * are unchanged.
 */

 * Deliveries of the IRQ go to the handler with its argument from the
